About the chef

Flavor-first cooking, built for a healthier life.

Chef Marvin Jones brings a soulful, ingredient-led approach to everyday meals—showing that meaningful flavor does not need salt to be memorable.

Our mission

Cooking with intention, not compromise.

The UNSALT'D Life began with a simple question: what if healthier food could still feel indulgent? For Chef Marvin, that answer became a culinary philosophy grounded in aroma, acid, texture, and balance.

With years of experience in restaurant kitchens and private dining, Marvin developed a salt-free approach that leans into herbs, citrus, char, roasted vegetables, smoked spices, fermented ingredients, and layered techniques that keep each plate vibrant and satisfying.

Today, his work spans private dining, tailored meal planning, cooking classes, and branded culinary partnerships—all built around the idea that healthy eating should be exciting, restorative, and delicious.

Core values

Built around better habits and better flavor.

Ingredient-led

Every dish starts with quality produce, layered aromatics, and texture that naturally creates depth.

Whole-body wellness

We cook for energy, longevity, and vitality without sacrificing the joy of eating well.

Accessible luxury

Healthy food should feel elevated, but also practical and approachable for real life.
